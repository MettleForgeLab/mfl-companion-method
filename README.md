# Mettle Forge Lab Companion Method

A companion file prepares the chatbot someone already uses to help them explore a particular place, project, institution, or body of work.

It brings together substantive knowledge, useful relationships, routes to sources, and guidance for working with the person's questions. The recipient can inspect the preparation, supply it to their chosen assistant, and begin wherever their interest lies.

Mettle Forge Lab combines this with a three-part presentation:

- A short message explains the human connection.
- A PDF gives the reader a composed encounter with the work.
- A companion supports further questions, investigation, and connections to the reader's circumstances.

The preparation travels with the person. They can leave a website and continue exploring the supplied material, follow a relationship into another public place, or bring the file into another conversation. A connected information environment becomes easier to approach without requiring every visitor to learn its whole organization.

MFL has implemented this arrangement in four accepted audience presentations sharing **Meet MFL edition 0.4**.
