# Source and release standing

**Method documentation:** version 1.0.0, prepared 18 November 2026 for publication at `MettleForgeLab/mfl-companion-method`.

**Method:** MFL's authored practice, documented here from existing companions, accepted presentations, their preparation and release records, and Porter's account of how the pieces cooperate.

**Example:** the personal PDF is accepted Edition 02; Meet MFL is accepted shared companion edition 0.4. Both retain their exact accepted content. The message is the existing generic personalization template. The repository ZIP is teaching packaging for the method and does not create a new edition of those works.

**Starter:** a public drafting aid, not an accepted universal companion format. **Worked journey:** an illustrative account using real materials, not observed reader-test evidence.

The completed MFL comparative reassessment remains closed. This repository documents the companion method and presentation arrangement; it does not replace that research or add a uniqueness finding.

The method version, companion edition, and PDF edition describe different objects. A method release may therefore remain v1.0.0 while Meet MFL remains edition 0.4 and the PDF remains Edition 02.
