MEET MFL - PERSONAL EXAMPLE
Teaching bundle for the MFL Companion Method, version 1.0.0.

MESSAGE.md is the generic message. Personalize its two placeholders when sending.
Meet_MFL_A_Look_Around.pdf is the accepted personal presentation, Edition 02.
Meet MFL.txt is the accepted shared companion, edition 0.4.

Read the PDF directly. If you would like to explore with your own chatbot:
1. Give it Meet MFL.txt.
2. Say: "Use this file as background. I'd like to explore [your interest]."
3. You can also add the PDF for its pictures and full presentation if your chatbot supports it.

This bundle illustrates the three-part arrangement. Its PDF, companion, and message wording preserve their accepted source content. The bundle itself is teaching packaging for the method; it is not a new edition of those works or a report of a receiving trial.
