# A garden, a question, and a way onward

*Illustrative receiving journey using existing MFL material. This is not a transcript or a report of a reader test.*

A person receives Porter's personal introduction because its tools and visual work might interest them. The short message makes that connection and leaves the choice of where to look with the reader.

They open **Meet_MFL_A_Look_Around.pdf** and linger over Mabel's garden. The drawing, movement described in the presentation, and invitation into the website catch their attention.

They give **Meet MFL.txt** to their chatbot and ask:

> How does something like this get made—and could I use the approach in a project of my own?

The companion supplies enough background to discuss the relationship among visual composition, browser behavior, authored entrances, and retained teaching materials. The chatbot can begin with that account and identify which parts would need closer inspection.

The reader chooses to visit [Mabel's public entrance](https://mettleforgelab.com/#mabel). Seeing the work may answer the question, change it, or lead to another. A technical interest could lead to MFL's source environment and the [GitHub Companion](https://mettleforgelab.com/companions/read/github/). A question about the larger practice could stay with Meet MFL.

The chatbot should inspect the actual source before describing unseen code as verified. It can also say that a particular teaching package is retained for teaching rather than presenting it as a public download. Those distinctions make the next step more useful.

Later, the reader returns to their own project. They still have the saved companion and can continue while its content remains available to the conversation—or supply it again elsewhere. They might bring a colleague, compare another approach, or email Porter with a question that has become clearer.

There is no required final step. The recipient may simply have enjoyed a garden and understood something about its making. The same preparation also leaves a route toward deeper investigation or collaboration.
