# Receiving a companion

1. Obtain the companion text file. In the worked example it is named **Meet MFL.txt**.
2. Attach it to a chatbot you enjoy using. If attachments are unavailable, provide the text in a way that your chatbot supports.
3. Say that the file is preparation for exploring the subject, then ask what interests you.

For example:

> Use Meet MFL.txt as background for this conversation. I'm interested in the gardens and how they are made. Help me explore that.

You can add the PDF if you want the chatbot to use its pictures and full presentation, where the chatbot supports reading them. You can also read the PDF directly.

The chatbot remains your assistant. You can ask for sources, disagree, follow another interest, or stop when you have enough. Further files are optional.

Keep the companion if you want to use it again. Whether its contents remain available in an existing conversation depends on that service's context and file handling.
